# Open Source Legal editorial contributor agreement

Version 1

This agreement covers original articles, edits and directory entries you intentionally submit for publication in the Open Source Legal content repository. By signing, you agree to these terms for your present and future editorial contributions to this repository.

1. **Ownership and permission.** You retain ownership of your contribution. You confirm that you own the relevant rights or have authority from the rights holder, including your employer where applicable, to submit the contribution on these terms.
2. **Publication and reuse.** You offer your contribution to Open Source Legal and the public under the [Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en). The project may credit you using the byline or GitHub identity you provide.
3. **Third-party material.** Identify any third-party material, its source, its applicable license and any required attribution. Only submit material you have permission to include. Linked tools, datasets and third-party quotations retain their applicable terms.
4. **Signing.** Read the agreement and reply with the exact signing phrase provided by the CLA Assistant on your pull request. The repository records your GitHub identity, the pull request, the time of signature and the agreement version in its public signature branch. Each contributor signs for their own work.
