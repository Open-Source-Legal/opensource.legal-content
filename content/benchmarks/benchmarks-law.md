Start here if you are choosing what to measure. The editor round-trip trial follows a document through import and export and checks which features survive. The legal-AI studies ask different questions, including how reviewers score contract drafts. Each report identifies its own reference and scoring method. [Study catalog](https://benchmarks.law/)

**How to use the collection.** For a document editor, open a round-trip report and look up a feature your documents rely on. For drafting, open the linked study and inspect who evaluated the output and what they scored. Keep the report name with any result you cite. The site also hosts the interactive results for neurotic-docx-bench. [DOCX benchmark and harness](/benchmarks/neurotic-docx-bench)
