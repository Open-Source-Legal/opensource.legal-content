A BigLaw Bench task starts with work a lawyer could bill for. In the deal-points workflow, the system reads a share purchase agreement and extracts specified terms. In the Core collection, tasks range from diligence to litigation analysis. Harvey’s lawyers write rubrics that award credit for required elements and deduct points for errors. A percentage describes how much of that rubric an answer satisfies. [Method and examples](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/introducing-biglaw-bench)

## Available tasks and rubrics

Harvey publishes materials for Core, Workflows and Retrieval in its repository and asks readers to contact the team for the full dataset.
